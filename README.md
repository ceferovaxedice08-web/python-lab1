This lab is about solving the same cinema ticket problem in three ways: with loops, with functional tools, and with a callback.
In Task 1, I used a for loop and if/elif/else to find each price, and children under 12 and people aged 65 or more got a discount.
In Task 2, I used map(), filter() and reduce() without loops, and the total revenue was the same.
In Task 3, I wrote sell_ticket with a strategy callback, so I can use different price rules like regular, weekend and student day.
